Hover-persistent mega menu

Hover (or focus) Products or Solutions. The multi-column panel opens, and crucially it stays open while you move the cursor diagonally from the trigger down into the panel — a short close-delay plus a "hover intent" bridge prevents the flicker that naive :hover-only menus suffer. Keyboard users can Tab to a trigger and press Enter/Space or Arrow Down to open it, Escape to close.
